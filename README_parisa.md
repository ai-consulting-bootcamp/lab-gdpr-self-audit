# TestVet GDPR Audit Lab

This repository contains my deliverables for **Audit your own project — GDPR lens**. I audited my actual TestVet Project 3 from a GDPR and EU data-protection perspective.

## Files

| File | Purpose |
|---|---|
| `gdpr_audit_testvet.md` | My first-person GDPR audit of TestVet, including the data-processing brief, personal-data inventory, role map, lawful-basis assessment, risk and rights analysis, AI Act/ePrivacy/Data Act check, accountability gaps, compliance memo, and privacy-by-design checklist. |
| `README.md` | File map and submission notes for this lab. |

## Scope and limitations

I based my assessment on the public TestVet repository reviewed on 24 August 2026. My code does not document a production host, persistent storage, retention schedule, authentication, analytics, DPAs, or international-transfer mechanisms. I therefore marked those items as unknown or requiring legal review rather than guessing.

This is an educational first-pass assessment, not legal advice, a DPIA, a certification, or a claim of compliance.
